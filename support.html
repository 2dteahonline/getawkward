How do I play?

Add everyone's names, pick how long to play, and pass the phone. Each turn one player is in the spotlight: the host picks a section card, then a question, and the spotlight answers out loud. Awkward deals one question that can't be dodged. "How to play" on the home screen walks through it.

How do I turn the 18+ questions on or off?

Settings → Romance → 18+ questions. Off removes them; Romance still plays with the rest.

Questions keep repeating / I want old questions back

Questions you've played don't come back until a section runs out. To bring them all back now, use Settings → Reset seen cards.

My game disappeared

A game in progress is saved when you leave the app. Choose "pick up where we left off" on the home screen. Ending the game from the pause menu clears it.
