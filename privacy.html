Privacy Policy

Get Awkward · effective November 7, 2026

Short version: Get Awkward collects nothing. The app works entirely offline. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no network connections of any kind.

What stays on your phone

To work, the app saves a few things on your device only:

  • Player names you type, so they can be offered next time ("played before").
  • Which questions have already been asked, so they don't repeat too soon.
  • A game in progress, so you can pick up where you left off.
  • Your settings, such as whether 18+ questions are on.

None of this ever leaves your device. We can't see it, and it isn't shared with anyone. Deleting the app deletes all of it. You can also clear the asked questions in Settings.

What we collect

Nothing. We don't collect, store, sell or share any personal information or usage data. Apple may give us anonymous, aggregated App Store statistics (like download counts) as it does for every app; we never receive anything that identifies you.

18+ questions

Some questions in the Romance section are for adults. The app asks "Are you 18 or older?" before showing them. The answer is stored only on your device and can be changed in Settings.

Children

Get Awkward is not directed at children under 13, and it collects no information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
